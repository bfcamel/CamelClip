# Community conduct

Everyone participating in issues, pull requests and discussions should be treated respectfully. Discuss ideas and code; avoid personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, threats and disclosure of another person's private information.

Assume good intent, explain disagreements with evidence and accept constructive feedback. Help newcomers understand the project without judging their experience or language skills.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Report harmful behavior privately through the maintainer's available private contact channel; if none is listed, request one without disclosing identifying details publicly. Maintainers should apply these rules consistently and protect the privacy of reporters.
